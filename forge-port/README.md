# Forge 26.3 port workspace

This is an incomplete development scaffold, not a complete or validated mod. It targets Minecraft 26.3 / Forge 66.1.0 / Java 25. The reference source and original 1.20.1 JAR are in the parent directory.

The entity uses vanilla Wither movement in early stages, a simplified large-stage hover and chase, and a simplified nearby-target selector. It has a summoning pattern, synced and saved growth state, phase, subphase and consumption commands, temporary item attraction, simple single-block terrain absorption, and placeholder tractor beams for the central head in stages 2 and 3 and all three heads in stage 4 and later. Models include the original early-stage body geometry and several growth subphases, with approximate jaw and tentacle animations. Stages 4 through 7 and several intermediate forms have approximate original bodies, heads and tentacles; basic linked segment entities and mirrored segment geometry are present; segment targeting and tractor pulls are approximated; full combat, revival, damage and tearing states remain incomplete. Phase-dependent base collision dimensions and configurable stage 4+ damage immunity are present, but the large-entity movement and collision behavior remain unported. A partial Wither Sickness tracker and golden apple stew are included.

Full storm AI, accurate beam rendering and head control, terrain consumption and block clusters, later models and boss battles, and complete sickness behavior are not yet ported. Temporary chunk tickets expire automatically and do not restore unattended storms after server restarts. Item tracking saves item UUIDs, but unresolved targets expire after 60 seconds and full multi-block clusters are not implemented. No in-game testing has been performed.

The official Forge MDK is used for build configuration. Building with `./gradlew build` produces an incomplete development build until the gameplay systems have been migrated.

A preliminary placeable formidibomb, ignition, attraction and collapse/revival sequence are present. A temporary melee symbiont with an approximate charge-and-knockback pulse can spawn late in phase 5 and drops the command block book; its base model, texture, glowing eyes, tentacle animation and boss bar are present; its overlays, combat animations, other spells, pulse block effects and full summoning rules remain unported, Super TNT uses the original 320-tick fuse, power-32 explosion and chain ignition; the crafted formidibomb now retains its fuse in inventory, dropped items and placed blocks. Full segment combat and the internal encounter remain unported. Segment creation requires the destination chunks to be loaded, and persistent loading across restarts is not yet implemented. Fallen storms can revive via `/witherstormmod revive` or after a configurable timer (60 minutes by default).
